## Ingredients

- 1 pound boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1/2-inch cubes (2 lb.)
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 1-1/2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon canola oil
- 2 cans (15-1/2 ounces each) great northern beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 can (14-1/2 ounces) chicken broth
- 2 cans (4 ounces each) chopped green chilies
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (add 1/2 this amount for less kick)
- 1 cup (8 ounces) sour cream (hold for dairy free)
- 1/2 cup heavy whipping cream (substitute 1 can full fat coconut milk)
- 1 large handful of chopped cilantro
- Tortilla chips, optional
- Shredded cheddar cheese, optional
- Sliced seeded jalapeño pepper, optional

## Directions

1. In a large saucepan, saute the chicken, onion and garlic powder in oil until chicken is no longer pink. Add the beans, broth, chilies and seasonings. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat; simmer, uncovered, for 30 minutes.

2. Remove from the heat; stir in sour cream and cream. If desired, top with tortilla chips, cheese and jalapeños.

## Notes

- For **dairy free**: hold the sour cream and substitute 1 can full fat coconut milk for the heavy whipping cream.
- For **less heat**: use only 1/8 teaspoon cayenne pepper instead of 1/4.
